Entry is the price when each call was posted: the last daily close before the post, or the price stated in the alert where it gave one. Stop is the published invalidation; where none was published, it is the prior weekly candle's low for a long or high for a short. Stops and T1 count as hit on a touch of the daily high or low since the post. Prices are Yahoo Finance daily data. This page loads the latest saved results each time it opens. "R total" is the result in units of risk (the distance from entry to stop). Once T1 is touched, half the position is closed at T1 and the stop on the other half moves to entry; if price later touches entry, the runner closes at zero. Calls that have not hit T1 are still open with their full stop.

Every call on this board was posted live, before the move. Entry, stop and targets for every call go to paid subscribers. Free subscribers get the write-ups and these results.
